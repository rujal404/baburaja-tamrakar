Babu Metal Crafts

Welcome to Babu Metal Crafts — "Where Tradition is Shaped into Timeless Copper & Brass Masterpieces"

Nestled in the cultural heart of Patan, Babu Metal Crafts is a proud family-owned business passed down through generations of skilled artisans. Rooted in heritage and refined by experience,
we specialize in customized, tailor-made copper and brass products crafted to match your exact vision and needs.

Every piece we create reflects dedication, precision, and authentic Nepalese craftsmanship. From decorative masterpieces and religious artifacts to functional metal creations for hotels, restaurants, and corporate spaces,
our work blends traditional artistry with modern refinement.

Trusted by over 10,000 customers, our reputation has been built on quality, reliability, and lasting relationships. We are honored to serve thousands of satisfied clients, including hospitality businesses and organizations that value excellence in every detail.

Our mission is simple — to deliver the highest quality tailor-made products that perfectly meet your unique preferences. Through craftsmanship, innovation, and unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction,
we strive to exceed expectations and create metal artistry that stands the test of time.

At Babu Metal Crafts, we don’t just create products — we craft legacy, trust, and timeless beauty in copper and brass.

Babu Metal Crafts is a traditional metal art studio in Nepal, admired by customers worldwide
who value timeless design and genuine handmade artistry.
